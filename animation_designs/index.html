Animation Designs

Cartoon-style activity indicators for Excalidraw elements

Node State Animations

Five lifecycle states for AI nodes on the canvas — Sleeping, Editing, Working, Done, Broken. Two style variants each, plus an interactive DAG showcase. Click to explore.

5 states / interactive

Working / Running

01 — Fuming (Ed, Edd n Eddy)

Jittery squiggly lines rising above the element. Classic cartoon "anger fumes" with randomized bezier curves that regenerate each frame.

sketchy / chaotic

02 — Electric Sparks

Lightning bolts crackling from the edges with small spark marks scattered around. Branching bolts with optional glow effect.

energy / power

03 — Heat Waves

Smooth sine waves rising upward, fading as they climb. Like heat shimmer off hot asphalt. Continuous motion with per-wave phasing.

smooth / ambient

04 — Dust Puffs

Particle-based cartoon smoke clouds that spawn, rise, expand, and fade out. Bumpy cloud shapes with organic movement.

playful / soft

05 — Pulse Rings

Expanding wobbly rounded rectangles that pulse outward from the element and fade. Like a heartbeat or sonar ping.

clean / rhythmic

06 — Scribble Vibrate

The rectangle itself shakes — the outline redraws with heavy jitter each frame, plus scratchy emphasis lines radiating from edges.

intense / frantic

07 — Gear Cogs

Hand-drawn rotating gears meshing together above the element. Mechanical "working" metaphor with wobbly tooth profiles.

mechanical / deliberate

Initializing / Booting Up

08 — Drawing Itself In

The rectangle sketches itself into existence edge by edge, with a pencil dot at the drawing tip. Fill and label fade in as it completes.

constructing / appearing

09 — Flicker On

Old fluorescent light turning on — the element flickers in and out with increasing stability until it locks solid. Glitch variant adds scan bars and static.

electric / unstable

10 — Winding Up

Spring coils, clockwork keys, and orbiting dots that accelerate and tighten as initialization progresses. Mechanical anticipation.

mechanical / building

11 — Filling Up

The rectangle fills from bottom to top — liquid with wavy surface and bubbles, digital scan lines, or organic ink bleed spreading upward.

progress / loading

12 — Loading Dots

Classic cartoon dots — bouncing, typewriter-stamping, or thought-bubble pulsing. Simple, universally understood "thinking" indicator.

classic / minimal
