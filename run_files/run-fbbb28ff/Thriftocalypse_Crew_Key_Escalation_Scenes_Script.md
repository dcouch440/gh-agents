# Thriftocalypse Crew vs. Baron von Blunder: Key Escalation Scenes Script

Superhero parody comedy script. 6 pivotal scenes escalating from accidental assembly to chaotic victory. Relentless puns, visual gags, snappy banter. Standard format. Each ~250-350 words.

## SCENE 1: Accidental Assembly Amid Aisle Antics (Beat 2)
**INT. GIANT THRIFT EMPORIUM - AISLES - NIGHT**

The thrift aisles overflow with bins of mismatched socks, faded tees, and mystery stains. Chaos echoes from the dance floor: *SLIP! SPLAT! WHOOPEE FART!*

SOCKTOPUS rummages a sock bin, his left-sock cape wafting funky fumes. Tentacles of thread snag a lone argyle.

                    SOCKTOPUS
        Dryer hitmen strike again! The pair knows
        the truth—left socks never die!

MUSTARD MAULER critiques condiment packets in his yellow hazmat suit, squirting a tester.

                    MUSTARD MAULER
        This dijon? Amateur hour. Needs relish-able
        zest for true anarchy!

BACKWARD BLASTER Heelys-reverses into a shelf, tracksuit zipper dangling comically from crotch.

                    BACKWARD BLASTER
        I foresaw this... tomorrow! Yellow peril
        slips the mustard seat.

He crashes into PIGEON WHISPERER, who coos haiku amid pigeon flock.

                    PIGEON WHISPERER
        Feathers fly / Thrift throne trembles / Honk if
        you love me.

BARON VON BLUNDER *squeak-squeaks* in, Clumsiness Aura rippling banana peels. His doily cape snags Socktopus's tentacles.

                    BARON VON BLUNDER
        You sock-stealers ruined my rug empire!
        Taste banana splits!

Socktopus yanks—Baron slips, mustache droops. Mustard Mauler blasts mustard jets: peels become gooey "sundaes."

Pigeons poo-fog the monocle. *SPLAT!*

                    SOCKTOPUS
        Team-up thrift alert! $1 refunds or bust!

Crew assembles in sock-mustard-pigeon pile-up. Henchmen slip in. Absurdity escalates.

*FADE OUT.*

*FADE OUT.*
