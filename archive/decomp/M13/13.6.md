# Ticket 13.6: Polish & Animations

> Spinning indicators for busy agents, smooth progress bars, status change flashes, and loading/empty/error states.

## Goal

Visual polish that makes the Agent View feel alive: busy agents show a spinning indicator, progress bars animate smoothly, status changes flash briefly, and edge cases are handled gracefully.

**Checkpoint**: Busy agents spin, progress bars fill smoothly, status changes flash, loading/empty/disconnected states display correctly.
